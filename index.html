About: Who I am

Hi! I’m Sandra De Guzman, 27 years old and currently pursuing my degree. I started college a bit later than most due to personal matters, but I never gave up on my dream.

I was born on May 25, 1999, and I’m currently living in Bulan, Sorsogon, in the Bicol Region, Philippines. I’m single, and my zodiac sign is Gemini. I am the fourth in my family to go to college.

My goal in life is simple: to be happy. For me, that means finishing my studies and having my dream job. My dream is to become a successful businesswoman, earning enough to support my own needs and wants, and most importantly, to help my family.

I am currently taking a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Sorsogon State University – Bulan Campus. To me, Computer Science is not just about code, software, or technology. It’s about growing: learning what I’m capable of, being brave enough to face challenges, and slowly building the future I want for myself.

I’ve learned that success doesn’t happen overnight. It takes dedication, patience, and the courage to keep going, even when things get hard. Being a student taught me that patience can open doors that knowledge alone can’t. My time as a Supply Office Intern taught me how to be responsible, adaptable, and how to work with different kinds of people.

My experience in NSTP-CWTS also taught me the value of service and teamwork. Through community activities like clean-up drives and helping others, I learned to be disciplined, compassionate, and to care not only for myself but for my community. It reminded me that true success is not just about what I achieve, but also about how I help others.

Even so, I know I still have a lot to learn. I don’t have all the skills yet, and I don’t have all the answers. But I have the willingness to learn, the drive to work hard, and the openness to grow with every opportunity. Just like the code I write, I’m still a work in progress, and I’m proud of every step that brings me closer to who I want to be.
